Frequently asked questions

Who can join Funngro?

Funngro is built for young people. Check the official Funngro website for the current age and eligibility rules before you sign up.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Start with a skill you already enjoy and choose a small project first. Each finished project adds to your experience.

How do I get paid?

Each project lists its own terms. Read the brief carefully before you apply so you know what is expected and what you will earn.

Should I tell my parents or guardians?

Yes. Talk to a parent or guardian about online work and review Funngro's official policies together.

Which skills can I use?

Popular starting points are web design, video editing, content writing and app or website testing.
